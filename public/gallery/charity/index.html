Prologue · A Word Before the Works

F
rom the shores of Kerala, India, Pastor Peter George and Pastor Asha George answered a call that carried them to Uganda, East Africa. There, compassion took the shape of bread for the hungry, medicine for the sick, shelter for the orphaned, and dignity for the forgotten. What follows are four windows into a far greater work — a ministry sustained not by wealth, but by faith, and carried forward one act of grace at a time.
"Religion that God our Father accepts as pure and faultless is this: to look after orphans and widows in their distress." — James 1:27
