Prologue · The Story Behind the Works

F
rom the shores of Kerala, India — moved by an unquenchable fire — Pastor Peter George and Pastor Asha George crossed continents to serve the people of Uganda, East Africa. What began as a single act of obedience became a living tapestry of grace: churches planted, children sheltered, the sick healed, women empowered, and communities reborn. This book is their testimony. These pages hold what words alone cannot — the holy labour of a ministry that refused to settle for the ordinary when God called them to the extraordinary.
"He is the one we proclaim, admonishing and teaching everyone with all wisdom, so that we may present everyone fully mature in Christ." — Colossians 1:28
